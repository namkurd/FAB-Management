How this is scored
Points above replacement (PAR)
Same definition as the Trade Network. Started PAR = for every regular-season week the pickup was in your starting lineup, his points minus a replacement-level player's points per game: PAR = pts − replacement PPG × games started. Starting a dud or an injured player counts against you. Rostered PAR counts every week he was on your roster and played, started or not (bye and injury weeks skipped) — talent-spotting rather than usage.
Replacement level
Per season and position: the points per game of the player ranked at a cutoff. Cutoff = how many of that position managers actually started each week (from real lineups, so flex and superflex count) + bench depth (½ per team at QB, TE, K, DEF; 1 per team at RB, WR). Every NFL player is ranked by PPG in this league's scoring over completed regular-season weeks (≥ 40% of games played). The tables are read straight from the Trade Network so both tools use identical numbers; the live season's cutoffs move as weeks are played.
Each cell: cutoff rank · replacement PPG. Hover for the player at the cutoff.
PAR / week
Pickup PAR ÷ regular-season weeks played: how many points a week the wire added to your lineup beyond a freely available player. Fair across seasons, including the partial one.
Early calls and chases
Timing compares the player's PAR per game in the 3 weeks before the pickup counted with the 4 weeks after (league scoring, every NFL player). Early: replacement-level or worse before (≤ 0), startable after (≥ +3 PAR/G), and you held him for at least 2 of those weeks. Chase: one spike game right before the pickup (≥ +8 above replacement), nothing sustained before it (the 3 weeks prior at or below replacement, or no games), and at or below replacement in the 4 weeks after.
Difference-makers
Pickups worth 25+ started PAR.
Regret and Net
Regret = the started PAR players you dropped went on to produce in other managers' lineups that season (players who were started elsewhere below replacement count as 0). Net = pickup PAR − regret.
PAR per FAB $
PAR from claims that cost $1 or more ÷ the dollars spent on those claims. $0 claims are left out.
Limits
Sleeper only shows winning bids, so there's no read on overpaying vs. the market. PAR is hindsight value. Playoff weeks aren't counted.
