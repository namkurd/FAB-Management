    How this is scored
    Started points (headline)
    Points the added player scored in that manager's starting lineup, regular season only, from the week of the add until he left the roster. Benched weeks count 0. Points come straight from Sleeper's matchup data, so they use the league's exact scoring.
    Rostered points
    Same window, counting every week he was on the roster whether started or not — pure talent-spotting. The add week only counts if the move happened before that week's first kickoff (or the player was started).
    Regret / Net
    Regret is what a dropped player went on to score in other managers' starting lineups for the rest of that regular season. Net = started points of the add minus regret of the player dropped in the same transaction.
    Hit rate
    Share of completed adds that produced at least 30 started points.
    Claim success
    Completed FAAB claims ÷ (completed + failed). "Outbid" failures = another team won the player; "Roster" failures = the claim would have left too many players on the roster.
    Early / Chase
    Timing uses league-scoring points per game for every NFL player (Sleeper weekly stats × league scoring settings). Early: low output in the 3 weeks before the add, startable output in the 4 weeks after (threshold 12 PPG; 18 for QB, 10 for K/DEF). Chase: added after a big stretch, then fell off.
    Seasons
    Regular season ends at week 15 through 2025 and week 14 from 2026. Playoff started points are tracked separately and not in the headline. The in-progress season only includes completed weeks; "All" totals include it, so compare per-season and per-add columns when mixing in a partial year.
    Limits
    Sleeper only exposes winning bids, so overpay vs. the market can't be measured. Points are hindsight value, not what a pickup looked like at the time.
